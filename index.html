About Mardiya

Hiya I am Mardiya, a mummy of 11 children and yes I gave birth to all 11 of them...

From a young age, I have always loved writing. Growing up in the Netherlands, I used to write poems in Dutch, simply because I enjoyed putting feelings and emotions into words.

As life became more expensive and raising a large family came with many responsibilities, I decided to return to something I had always loved: writing poems.

Now I use that passion to help other people express their love, gratitude and emotions through meaningful words, beautiful designs and framed keepsakes that can be treasured for years.

Each creation is made with care, sincerity and from the heart.
