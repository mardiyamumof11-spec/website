About Mardiya

Hiya, I'm Mardiya, a proud mummy of 11 and the person behind every personalised poem and design you see here.

My love of writing began in the Netherlands, where I first wrote poems in Dutch. I discovered how a few carefully chosen words could make someone smile, offer comfort or express feelings that are not always easy to say out loud.

Every poem begins with someone's story.

Today, I help people turn their own memories, feelings and special moments into meaningful poems and beautiful keepsakes. It might be a wedding, a new baby, a heartfelt thank you, a celebration or a loving tribute to someone dearly missed.

No two stories are the same, and that is what makes every poem special.

I take time to listen to what matters to you, whether that is a little memory, a favourite quality or words you have always wanted to say. I write each poem personally and create its design with care, sincerity and attention to detail.

I listen with care, write from the heart, and turn those precious feelings into words they can treasure forever.

Thank you for trusting me with the moments and people who mean so much to you. 🩷
