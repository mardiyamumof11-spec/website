A Little About Me ~ Motherhood Through Every Stage 🩷🩵

Hiya, amazing mums and mums to be. I'm Mardiya a proud mother of 11 children.

I always say that I'm experiencing three generations of motherhood all at once!

Read My StoryClose My Story+

I have grown up children finding their own way in life, younger children approaching their teenage years and 4 little ones under the age of six.

From newborn cuddles to conversations with my adult children, motherhood has been my greatest journey and one of my biggest teachers.

Over the years, I've experienced pregnancy, childbirth, natural births, C sections and inductions, followed by the newborn stage again and again.

11 children, 11 different personalities and 11 unique journeys. And believe me, no two experiences have ever been exactly the same...

I've known the excitement of expecting a baby, the endless questions, the sleepless nights, the beautiful first moments and the challenges nobody quite prepares you for.

I'm still learning every day and I certainly don't claim to have all the answers. But I will always do my very best to listen, encourage and share what I've learned along the way.

I have years of real life experience, a listening ear and a heart that understands how overwhelming motherhood can sometimes feel.

That's why I created Mother to Mother Chats.

Sometimes you don't need another textbook or a long list of instructions. Sometimes you simply want to talk to a mother who's been there, who understands and who will listen without judgement.

Alongside motherhood, I also create personalised poems and designs, turning life's precious moments into beautiful keepsakes.

Eleven children. Many years of memories. One mother, sharing from the heart🩷🩵
