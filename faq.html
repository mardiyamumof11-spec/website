Questions

A little help with your personalised poems and designs.

How long does it take?

+

Most personalised poems are completed within 1–3 working days.

Need it sooner? A 24 Hour Priority Service is available for an additional £10 (subject to availability). Please contact me before ordering so I can confirm I have availability.

Please note: The 24 hour service is not available on Sundays.

Can I request changes?

+

Yes! You'll receive a preview before your order is finalised. Small changes can be made to ensure you're completely happy before your design is sent or printed.

Can I have it framed?

+

Yes. You can choose either:

  • Digital Design (sent by email)
  • Framed Print (beautifully presented and ready to gift)

Do you post across the UK?

+

Yes. I post framed orders throughout the UK. Collection in Northampton may also be available by prior arrangement.

What occasions do you write for?

+

I create personalised poems for a wide range of occasions, including:

  • Weddings
  • Anniversaries
  • Birthdays
  • New Babies
  • Mother's Day
  • Father's Day
  • Memorials
  • Graduations
  • Retirements
  • Thank You Gifts
  • Islamic occasions, including Nikah, Eid, Hajj, Umrah, Aqiqah and more.

As a Muslim owned business, I warmly welcome customers of all backgrounds while only accepting requests that are in keeping with my Islamic values.
